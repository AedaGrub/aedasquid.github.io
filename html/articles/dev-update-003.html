Dev Update #3: The Conflict Outside

Posted on 23rd March 2025

War Beyond The Walls

If you're curious about the setting of the game, well, me too. Basically, there is a world war that threatens the extinction of humanity. So much damage has been done to the earth that nature has retreated into a permanent state of preservation, hence the autumn trees.

Did you know that deciduous trees (like maple trees) gather energy from their leaves and into the rest of their body to survive the cold winter? They lose their chlorophyllic colour as the method of survival is no longer generating energy but preserving it.

Now imagine a world where there is that constant threat of an eternal winter, and let all the trees brace futilely against that thought.

That's what the setting of the game is. A small solace from the ruins of conflict, just trying to survive and find purpose and happiness.
