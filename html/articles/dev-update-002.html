Dev Update #2: Origins of Autumn

Posted on 2nd March 2025

The Bright Orange

Originally, the game this website was going to be about my group's FYP game. However, we didn't have enough content or proper media to make a proper mockup of a landing page with it. As such, this fake game is derived from the first image in the design proposal.

I like Autumn and orange. Nothing else to it. I really liked the art by Bastien Grivet, and to keep the rest of the website homogenous, most of the images used are from his Autumnal works.
