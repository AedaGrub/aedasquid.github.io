Dev Update #4: Amelia of Rockharbour

Posted on 25th April 2025

Orphan or Survivor

The girl depicted in these autumnal illustrations is of Bastien Grivet's original creation. She seems to wander around a lot, interacting mostly with mechanical creatures. Perhaps there's few others in this world. Perhaps she just doesn't approach people.

That's an answer only Grivet would know.

Truthseeker

I've ran out of speculative content to write, though I guess blogs are meant for rambling. Everything is based off pictures, and even the "trailer" for the game is just a timelapse of Grivet's work.

Imagine there being discussions about mechanics and lore in these dev blogs. Imagine the Media page having in-game screenshots.

I hope you enjoyed reading these posts, if you even did. It isn't serious, and I kinda enjoyed the process.

Funnily enough, doing this CSS work has helped me on Unreal Engine and Unity's UI, as it's mostly style sheets as well. I can now see boxes.
